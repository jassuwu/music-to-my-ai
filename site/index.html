music to my ai

your ai talks, this plays it. one note per chunk of the reply, nothing to configure.

ai

it runs on claude.ai and chatgpt.com, which are hand-tuned, plus chatjimmy, gemini, t3.chat, deepseek, grok, perplexity and mistral, which share a reader that waits for a stream to prove itself before it plays anything. six voices, a volume slider, mute on ⌘⇧M. that's the whole popup.

it collects nothing, sends nothing, and makes no network requests. the samples ship inside it.
